First Law – Inertia

ΣF = 0 ⇒ v = constant

An object stays at rest, or moves in a straight line at constant speed, unless a net external force acts on it.

Key ideas

  • Inertia = resistance to change of motion
  • Mass measures inertia
  • Friction is a force—not a “need” for motion
🚀

Second Law – F = ma

ΣF = m a = dp/dt

The acceleration of a body is directly proportional to the net force and inversely proportional to its mass, along the force direction.

Key ideas

  • 1 N = 1 kg·m/s²
  • Double the force → double the acceleration
  • Double the mass → half the acceleration
🤝

Third Law – Action–Reaction

F₁₂ = −F₂₁

When body A exerts a force on body B, B exerts an equal and opposite force on A. The pair acts on different bodies.

Key ideas

  • Forces never cancel (different bodies)
  • Momentum of an isolated system is conserved
  • Lighter body gets larger acceleration

🛑 Inertia in daily life

  • 🚌 Passengers lurch forward when a bus brakes suddenly
  • 🪑 Pulling a tablecloth quickly leaves the dishes in place
  • 🧯 Dust flies off a carpet when beaten
  • 🛰️ Satellites coast in space with almost no friction
  • 🪢 Seat belts and headrests protect against inertia

🚀 F = ma in action

  • 🚗 A loaded truck accelerates slower than a car with the same engine force
  • 🏏 A cricketer “gives” with the ball to lengthen contact time and reduce force
  • 🚀 Rocket acceleration rises as fuel burns (mass falls)
  • 🏋️ Pushing an empty vs a full shopping cart
  • 🛗 Apparent weight change in a lift

🤝 Action–Reaction

  • 🚀 Rocket pushes gas down; gas pushes rocket up
  • 🏊 Swimmer pushes water back, water pushes swimmer forward
  • 🔫 Recoil of a gun when fired
  • 🦶 Walking: foot pushes ground back, ground pushes you forward
  • 🎈 Released balloon zooms around
